8:
I M Poster
Cara Benedetto
June 6, 2025
gallery text




A one night installation in which a performer stood in the corner in proximity to two small invitations:

"To replace Artist or Woman or Dog / please stand on the red line / and wait for tit"

and

"yr want is a gift / quiet and cornered / sit down + wait for tit".

Two sets of laser-engraved dog treats were made available to the audience: a cheddar sage and a chocolate chip cookie. From a set of stereo bookshelf speakers, three karaoke versions of White Town's 1997 hit "Your Woman" played on repeat for the duration of the night. Doggy bags were made available for taking treats home. Throughout the evening, audience participants joined the performer by standing in the corner or on the line, facing the wall and waiting. An hour and a half into the event, the lights turned off and remained off until the end of the night.
